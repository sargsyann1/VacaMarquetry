The Work, in Full
Concept

Anatomy as Structure

A geometric anatomical heart, built entirely from precision-cut wood veneer. The piece sits at the intersection of organic subject and mathematical fragmentation — natural material used as a structural language for the human form, rather than a literal likeness.

Process

95 Pieces, One Panel

Ninety-five individually cut veneer pieces converge into a single 32 × 42 cm panel, finished with the same matte acrylic varnish sealant used across the collection.

Purpose

 

“Part of the VaCa Marquetry Collection series — made for brand expression, gallery presentation, and architectural storytelling, not for sale.”

Collection Status

Anatomical Heart is a VaCa Marquetry Collection piece, held for brand expression and exhibition rather than acquisition. It does not carry a Certificate of Authenticity and is not available for shipping or sale.

Exhibition & Presentation

Shown as part of the studio's collection presentation and architectural storytelling — a milestone piece in the evolution of VaCa Marquetry's design language.
