The Work, in Full
Concept

Authority Without Declaration

The largest and most complex work in the collection. More than 910 individual pieces converging into a portrait of uncommon stillness. The Sovereign does not ask to be noticed. The scale is not for spectacle — it is for the quality of silence that only a large work can produce in a room.

Process

910+ Pieces, Six Weeks

Production extended over six weeks — the longest sustained build in the studio's history. Twelve veneer species were sourced for this single panel, including burled walnut, quilted maple, rosewood, and satinwood. The background recedes not through value contrast but through grain direction alone — an effect that required dismantling and rebuilding the lower third twice before it resolved.

Reflection
