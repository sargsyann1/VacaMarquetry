A Wood Portrait from Your Photograph, Cut Entirely by Hand

Hundreds of pieces of real wood veneer, each chosen for its colour and grain, cut freehand with a knife and fitted together until your photograph becomes a work of art.

Begin Your Portrait How It Works ↓

Not engraved. Not printed. Built.

Search for a “wood portrait from a photo” and almost everything you will find is made by a machine: a photograph burned into a board by a laser, or printed onto a wooden panel. Both can be charming. Neither is what we make.

A marquetry portrait is constructed, not reproduced. The face, the hair, the light on a cheekbone are each a separate piece of natural wood veneer, chosen from species such as walnut, oak, maple, ash, wenge and mahogany for the tone it carries, then cut by hand and fitted against its neighbours. There is no paint and no pigment: every shade you see is the natural colour of the wood.

Every portrait is cut freehand by the artist, Sasun Sargsyan, in his studio in Pisa, Tuscany. No laser, no template, no assistant. Because the image is rebuilt by hand rather than copied, no two portraits are ever the same, even from the same photograph.

Your Photograph

Which photo works best?

The portrait is only as expressive as the photograph it begins with, so a few minutes choosing the right one pays off.

  • Sharp and well lit. Soft daylight from a window is ideal; avoid harsh flash and deep shadow across the face.
  • Eyes in focus. The eyes carry the likeness. If they are sharp, almost everything else can be interpreted.
  • The original file. Send the photo straight from the phone or camera rather than a screenshot or a compressed copy from a messaging app.
  • One clear subject. Couples, families and groups are possible too; we will advise on the composition in your preview.

You can send up to five photographs. If you are unsure, send them all and let us suggest the strongest.
