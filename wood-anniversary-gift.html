The Wood Anniversary · Five Years

The Fifth Anniversary Is Wood. Make It a Portrait.

Five years of marriage are traditionally marked with wood: a material that grows stronger with time. A portrait of the two of you, cut by hand from natural veneers, is the most personal way to honour it.

Begin Your Portrait How It Works ↓

Why wood for five years?

In the traditional list of anniversary gifts, the fifth year belongs to wood. The meaning is easy to read: after five years a marriage has put down roots. It has weathered a few seasons and is still growing, slowly and solidly, ring by ring.

Most wooden anniversary gifts are objects: a box, a board, an engraved keepsake. A marquetry portrait is something else. It is a work of art made of wood itself, in which your wedding photograph, a favourite picture of the two of you, or the home you have built together is recreated from hundreds of hand-cut pieces of natural veneer.

There is a quieter symbolism too. A marquetry picture holds together because every piece is cut to fit the ones around it, exactly. It is a fair description of five years together.

Ideas

What to portray

  • Your wedding photograph. The obvious choice, and often the most moving one.
  • The two of you today. A favourite recent picture shows who you have become in five years.
  • The home you share, or the place where you met, married or spent your honeymoon.
  • The whole family, including children or the dog who completes the household.
  • A symbol of your own, like the rose or the heart: a single motif in fine woods.
Timing

When to order

A marquetry portrait cannot be rushed. Allow three to six weeks for the cutting, plus delivery: ideally, enquire about two months before the anniversary. The portrait form has a “Needed by” field, so tell us the date and we will tell you honestly whether it can be met.

Short on time? The preview itself makes a beautiful gift: give it on the day, and let the finished portrait follow.
