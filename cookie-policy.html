Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies (browser “local storage” and “session storage”) used on vacamarquetry.shop. For how we handle personal data in general, see our Privacy Policy.

1. Your cookie choice

On your first visit a banner lets you Accept or Decline analytics and advertising-measurement cookies. Nothing in category 4 below is activated unless you accept; the first-party attribution storage in category 3 stays in your browser and is only sent with an enquiry. You can change your choice at any time:

Your previous choice has been cleared. The cookie banner will appear again when this page reloads.

2. Necessary and functional storage

Used to make the site work and to remember your choice. It does not require consent.

NameTypePurposeDuration
vaca_cookie_consentLocal storageRemembers whether you accepted or declined cookiesUntil you clear it or change your choice

3. Enquiry attribution (first-party)

Stored only in your browser by our own website. These values help us understand which page or campaign led to an enquiry. They are sent to us only when you submit a form or click a WhatsApp button, together with that enquiry.

NameTypePurposeDuration
vaca_attributionLocal storageLanding page, referring site, campaign parameters (utm_*) and ad click identifiers (gclid, gbraid, wbraid, fbclid) from the links you followedUntil you clear your browser storage
vaca_visitor_idLocal storageRandom identifier that links repeat visits from the same browserUntil you clear your browser storage
vaca_session_id, vaca_session_start, vaca_pages_visitedSession storageIdentifies the current visit and counts pages viewedUntil you close the tab
vaca_intent_score, vaca_lead_source, vaca_page_visited, vaca_interaction_type, vaca_last_artworkSession storageWhich sections and artworks you viewed during the visit, used to pre-fill and prioritise your enquiryUntil you close the tab

4. Analytics and advertising measurement (only with your consent)

NameProviderPurposeDuration

These services are provided by Google; seeHow Google uses information from sites that use its services. Until you accept, Google tags run in a restricted, cookieless mode (Google Consent Mode).

5. Third-party services

Clicking a WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp (Meta), and links to Instagram, Facebook or YouTube open those services. Their own cookie policies apply once you leave our website.

6. Managing storage in your browser

You can delete cookies and local storage for this site at any time in your browser settings. Doing so resets your cookie choice and the attribution data described above.
